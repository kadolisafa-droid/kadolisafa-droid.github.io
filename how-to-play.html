How to Play Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe is played by two people on a board with three rows and three columns. One player uses X and the other uses O. Players take turns choosing empty squares until someone makes a line of three or the board is full.

Rules of the game

  1. Player X starts the first round.
  2. Choose any empty square to place your symbol.
  3. Players alternate turns between X and O.
  4. Try to make a straight line of three matching symbols.
  5. Block the other player when they are close to completing a line.
  6. If all nine squares are filled and nobody has a line of three, the round is a draw.

How do you win?

A winning line can run across any row, down any column or diagonally. Complete any one of the eight possible lines with your symbol before your opponent does.

Top row1 – 2 – 3
Middle row4 – 5 – 6
Bottom row7 – 8 – 9
Left column1 – 4 – 7
Middle column2 – 5 – 8
Right column3 – 6 – 9
Diagonal1 – 5 – 9
Diagonal3 – 5 – 7

Numbering starts at the top-left square and continues across each row.

Simple strategy tips

Look at the whole board before every move. The centre square is part of four possible winning lines, while each corner belongs to three. These positions can offer useful opportunities, depending on the marks already placed.

Watch for an opponent who has two matching marks and one empty square in a line. Blocking that open square can prevent an immediate loss. You can also look for a fork: a move that creates two different chances to win on your next turn.

Take your time, check the rows, columns and diagonals, and think about both your next move and your opponent's possible response.
