Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information this Tic Tac Toe website handles when visitors browse the pages and play the game.

Information you enter

The game does not ask visitors to create an account, provide a name or submit personal details to play. It is a two-player game on one device. The game does not send the board or scoreboard to a game server.

Information saved in your browser

This version uses your browser's local storage to remember the Player X score, Player O score, number of draws, which player should start the next round and whether sound is on or off. These settings stay in the browser on that device. They are not synced between devices. Clearing the browser's site data removes them.

Cookies and hosting information

The game code does not itself create advertising cookies. Like other websites, the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, requests and security logs to deliver and protect the site. The hosting provider's own privacy terms apply to that processing.

Advertising and third-party services

Advertising is not part of the game logic. If an advertising service such as Google AdSense is added to this website in the future, that provider and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies and process information such as device identifiers or IP addresses to serve, measure or personalise ads, subject to the provider's settings and applicable consent requirements. This policy should be reviewed and updated whenever advertising, analytics or other third-party services are added.

For information about Google's partner services, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can also review Google Ads Settings.

Children's privacy

This is a general-audience casual game. The game itself does not intentionally request personal information from children. Any technical data handled by the hosting provider or any future third-party service is governed by that provider's policies and applicable settings.

Your choices

You can clear this website's local storage or site data in your browser settings to remove the saved game scores and preferences. You can also turn sound off using the sound button on the Play Game page.

Policy updates

This policy may change if the website adds new features or third-party services. The update date at the top should be changed whenever the policy is revised. This text is a website starting point and should be checked against the services actually used on the published site.
